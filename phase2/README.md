Every chunk ends by analysing everything that exists and **executing the notebooks on the server**, so the
results are saved inside them: `phase2/outputs/notebooks/*.ipynb`, also packed in `~/phase2_results.tgz`.
Download that file, unpack it, and open the notebooks anywhere; no need to rerun anything.
`bash phase2/run_phase2.sh notebooks` re-does only the analysis and the notebooks (e.g. after Phase 1 finishes).
Restarting a chunk skips finished runs. Progress: `tail -f phase2/outputs/run_phase2.log`.

## Design decisions

- **Same streams for every method**: no-drift streams = the pre-switch triples in random order; injected
  streams = shuffled pre-switch triples, then post-switch triples, switch at a random position. Seeded per
  repeat, so methods are compared on identical streams.
- **Repeats**: fast methods 100 no-drift + 100 injected (measures) or 20 + 20 (Antoine's loop with a fixed
  embedding, all models). Methods that retrain need a training per new entity, so they get 10 + 10 with
  TransE and Hadamard only; Phase 1 shows all models and compositions behave alike under Antoine's method.
- **Retrain + align**: after every from-scratch re-embed, the new embedding (entities and relations) is
  rotated onto the previous one by orthogonal Procrustes on the shared entities.
- **Warm start**: the new model starts from the old vectors (matched by label) and keeps training. PyKEEN
  re-initialises weights at the start of training by default; this is switched off (tested).
- **Fold-in**: a new entity gets its own vector, fitted on the triple where it first appears; all old
  vectors stay exactly where they were (tested).
- **Two fixed-embedding variants**: `full` (trained on the whole cutoff graph; used for Antoine's loop, so
  only the re-embedding changes) and `holdout` (the 1,000 most recent monitored triples left out and used as
  the reference pool, so reference and stream are equally unseen; used for the measures).
- **Calibrated thresholds**: at most a 10% chance of any false alarm in 1,000 no-drift triples, from 200
  streams resampled from the calibration half of the reference (Cobb et al. 2022).
- **Threshold-free comparison**: probability that a measure's statistic within 500 triples after the switch
  exceeds its maximum over a whole no-drift stream (Hinder et al. 2024 use the same idea).
- **Embedding quality**: TransE MRR after 10-200 epochs; key comparisons repeated at 100 epochs.

In the CASE streams almost every disease first appears after the cutoff, so a fixed embedding has to fold
in most of them; this is also why Antoine's method re-embeds so often.

Not included (time): warm start inside the stream, gradual/subtle drift, a positive control, no-drift
streams in real time order, several seeds for everything.
